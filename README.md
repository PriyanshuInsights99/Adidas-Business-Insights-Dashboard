# 👟 Adidas Sales Analysis & Business Insights

### 📊 Power BI | SQL | Python | Business Intelligence

An end-to-end Adidas Sales Analysis project focused on analyzing sales performance, profitability, product contribution, retailer performance, geographical distribution, and sales channel effectiveness through data-driven business insights.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes Adidas sales data to identify business trends, evaluate revenue performance, understand product and retailer contributions, and generate actionable business recommendations.

The project combines data analysis, interactive visualization, and business reporting to transform raw sales data into meaningful insights.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profitability.
- Identify monthly sales trends and seasonal patterns.
- Evaluate geographical sales performance.
- Analyze product-wise revenue contribution.
- Compare retailer sales performance.
- Evaluate sales channels based on revenue and profitability.
- Generate actionable business insights and recommendations.

## 📈 Key Business Insights

- **Overall Sales:** Approximately $900 Million across all sales methods.
- **Top Sales Channel:** In-store generated $357M in sales.
- **Most Profitable Channel by Margin:** Online achieved a 46.42% profit margin.
- **Top Product:** Men's Street Footwear generated $209M in sales.
- **Top Retailer:** West Gear generated $243M in sales.
- **Top Sales State:** New York generated approximately $64.23M.
- **Top Sales Region:** West emerged as the highest-sales region in the geographical analysis.
- **Peak Sales Month:** July recorded the highest monthly sales of $23.1M.
